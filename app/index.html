x doubles back on itself, so this is a loop and not a function of x — a hysteresis loop, a forward-and-reverse sweep. Split it and the rising and falling branches are fitted separately, side by side.

How the fit is scored. With a σy column present, χ² = Σ (yi − f(xi))² / σi² and a good fit lands near χ²/ν ≈ 1. Without one, every point is given unit weight, so χ² is a plain sum of squared residuals and its absolute value carries no meaning — only the ranking does. ν = N − k. Parameter uncertainties come from the diagonal of the covariance matrix (JᵀWJ)−1, scaled by χ²/ν when no errors were supplied.

Why the free-form fit is handicapped. The symbolic search picks the best subset of a ~30-term dictionary, so it is choosing among thousands of candidate formulae — a form that wins on raw χ² alone is often just noise-fitting. With charge for the search on, it pays log₂(dictionary) effective parameters per term, so it only takes the top spot when it finds real structure the named families miss. Non-linear models are fitted directly in y-space by Levenberg–Marquardt, never by fitting a straight line to log-transformed data, so the χ² you see is the χ² of your actual measurements.

Combinations. Beyond the single families, the bench fits every sum of two components — peak plus sloping background, two peaks, trend plus oscillation, bi-exponential, and so on, each with an optional constant. Because a sum is linear in its amplitudes and nonlinear only in its shape parameters, the amplitudes are solved exactly at every step and only the shapes are searched (variable projection), which is what makes ~70 combinations affordable. A combination is charged log₂(number of combinations tried) effective parameters for having been picked, and any model left with fewer than 3 effective degrees of freedom is fitted and listed but never allowed to win — on a handful of points a 5-parameter sum fits anything.

Nothing is carried over between visits. Closing the page discards the loaded data and every setting; reopening starts at defaults with the sample set. Only History persists — it keeps your last 20 fits in this browser, and clicking one reloads its data, columns, ranges and equations exactly as they were. Nothing is ever sent anywhere.
